Why Sasarisa

Dictation apps turn speech into text. You still have to click, open, search and send — by hand.

Sasarisa finishes the job. Everyday things happen on your Mac in about a second; anything that needs thinking goes to the AI you use (Claude, Codex …), which looks at the screen and does it. It gets faster the more you use it.

How to call it

Call it, or hold a key and talk.

Rename it if you like. After the first call, keep talking for 30 seconds without the name.

“Sasarisa”or “Hey Sasarisa”

Talk after the chime. It acts within 0.9 seconds of you finishing.

Right ⌥ hold & talk

Like Handy: listens while held, runs on release. Most accurate in noisy rooms.

“That's all” · “Stop”

Cut in any time — even over speakers, if you talk close to the mic.

Safety

A voice in a video must never drive your Mac. Anything irreversible waits for your yes, and your voice never leaves the Mac.

Install

Ready in ten minutes.

Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) · macOS 14+ · one AI (a Claude or ChatGPT plan, an API key, or a free local model)

1

Connect one AI

Install Claude Code (Claude plan) or Codex (ChatGPT plan) and run “claude” or “codex” once in Terminal to log in. Aside, an API key or a local model can be picked in Settings → Brain.

2

Install Sasarisa

One line in Terminal — or “Download for macOS” above (notarized by Apple).

brew install --cask mvcompany8500-web/tap/sasarisa
3

Fix anything red on the setup screen

Allow the microphone, speech recognition and accessibility, and download the transcription model. When ◉ shows in the menu bar, say “Sasarisa”.

Windows comes later. The Mac comes first; a Windows version follows once wake-word listening and transcription there are as quick as on the Mac.
